4. Identification of site:

Rural Sports Centre should be established in any unobjectionable
Poromboke / Poromboke lands vested with Panchayats adjacent to the
habitations having the required extent of land.

The concerned Assistant Engineer / Junior Engineer / Overseer will inspect
the identified site and submit a feasibility report to the Block Development Officer
(VP). Officials from Revenue Department are to assist the RD&PR officials for the
site selection.

The Block Development Officer (VP) will consolidate the report along with
the approval of the Grama Sabha and to submit the proposal to obtain
administrative sanction by the District Collector for the establishment of Rural
Sports Centre.

Technical Specification:

e The site proposed should not be a low-lying or water-logged area. An
elevated surface shall be selected for the formation of the playground.

e Levelling the Ground surface by cutting /filling including compaction by
using power roller and it should be specifically mentioned in the estimate
and in the administrative sanction.

e Proper slope should be maintained so that the water gets drained off
easily.

e Brick pitching to be provided in the corners for Volleyball and Ball
Badminton court etc without projecting above ground level so as to
demarcate the boundary.

e Detailed Estimate along with Type Design/Drawing of Sports Ground will be
issued by Director, RD&PR

e The Assistant Engineer (RD) / Block Engineer shall ensure that the
technical specifications are adhered to and the estimate costs of the

proposed works have been correctly arrived at.

Non-Negotiable Items under MGNREGS

1. As per the Annual Master Circular 2022-23, only one play ground in a
Gram Panchayat can be constructed under Mahatma Gandhi NREGS. The
site in where sports ground established under MGNREGS or any other
scheme previously should not be selected again.

2. Citizen Information Board shall be kept at the work site in 3 X 4 feet with
all necessary information as per the framework given by the Government
of India vide Ref No: k11023 /1 / 12017 - MGNREGA (IV), MoRD, Dated
07.04.2017.

3. Geo Tagging of the Assets shall be done for all 3 stages namely Before,
During and After completion of the work.

4. MGNREGS work files (Checklist / Contents) as per the instructions of Gol,
vide Ref no: J11001017/6/12016 - MGNREGA (VII), MORD, Dt 21.12.2016
has to be maintained for all works.

5. The Rural Schedule of Rate finalised in G.O. Ms. No.65, RD&PR (CGS.1)
Department, Dated 21.04.2023 and G.O. Ms. No.74, RD&PR (CGS.1)

Department, Dated 05.06.2023 shall be adopted. In case of revision made
in Rural Schedule of Rates (RSoR) in future, the same shall be adopted.

6. Resolution should be obtained for in the Gram Sabha for all the selected
works and it should be entered work wise in Register No.2 of Mahatma
Gandhi NREGA of village panchayats concerned.

7. After Completion of Rural Sports Center, Detailed Documented report on
each school with High Resolution Photos (Before, During and After) and
Sports Events proposed to be conducted should be sent to Director,
RD&PR.
