2. In the Government order third read above, orders were issued
according permission to take up construction of 500 new Anganwadi Centers
at a total cost of Rs.70.00 crore and in the Government order fourth read
above, orders were issued according permission to take up certain works to
the tune of Rs.2,197.42 crore under MGNREGS 2023-2024.

3. In the letter fifth read above, the Director of Rural Development and
Panchayat Raj, has sent a proposal for the construction of 1,000 Nos, of Dug
Wells (750 Nos Individual and 250 Nos. Community) at a cost of
Rs.130.78 crore, construction of 200 Nos. of Food Grain Godowns (Model I -
More than 500 Card Holders) at a cost of Rs.25.34 crore and 200 Nos. of
Food Grain Godowns (Model II - Less than 500 Card Holders) at a cost of
Rs.18.64 crore, construction of 150 Nos. of Milk Producer’s Co-operative
Societies at a cost of Rs.30.20 crore and construction of 100 Nos. of 1.5m
Span Culverts, 100 Nos. of 3.00m Span Culverts and 100 Nos. of Minor
Bridges at a total cost of Rs.60.85 crore and requested the Government to
sanction a sum of Rs.54.35 crore towards State Share for the above works.

4, The Government have examined the proposal of the Director of
Rural Development and Panchayat Raj in detail and decided to accord
permission to take up the following works:-

a) Construction of 1,000 Nos. of Dug Wells (750 Nos Individual
and 250 Nos. Community) in rural areas at a cost of
Rs.130.78 crore;

b) Construction of 200 Nos. of Food Grain Godowns (Model I -
More than 500 Card Holders) in rural areas at a cost of
Rs.25.34 crore and 200 Nos. of Food Grain Godowns (Model II -
Less than 500 Card Holders) in rural areas at a cost of
Rs.18.64 crore;

c) Construction of 150 Nos. of Milk Producer’s Co-operative
Societies in rural areas at a cost of Rs.30.20 crore;

d) Construction of 100 Nos. of 1.5m Span Culverts, 100 Nos. of
3.00m Span Culverts and 100 Nos. of Minor Bridges as
standalone works in rural areas at a total cost of Rs.60.85 crore.

6. The General conditions for work selection and Non-Negotiable items for
the sanctioned works in para 4 above shall be as follows:-

(i) General Conditions for Work Selection

1.

2,

All works will be selected as per the GIS plan prepared for each village
panchayats.

Priority will be given to 7,705 Village Panchayats selected for the
implementation of Anaithu Grama Anna Marumalarchi Thittam-II and
Kalaignarin All Village Integrated Agriculture Development Programme.

. Beneficiary Selection for Dug wells will be done in consultation with

Agriculture/ Agriculture Engineering Department.

. The authenticity of Small/Marginal and SC/ST Farmers for providing

Dug wells will be duly verified with necessary certificates in
consultation with Revenue Department.

5. The site selection for Milk Producer’s Co-operative Society should
be done in consultation with Aavin and MPCS (Milk Producer’s
Co-operative Society)

6. The Site selection for Food Grain Godowns should be done in
consultation with Registrar of Co-operative Societies and Civil Supplies
Corporation.

7. Construction of cross drainage structures shall be selected on the
following basis (1) roads which are connecting habitations, (ii) Taluk
and Panchayat headquarters, (iii) roads linking to marketing centres,

_ educational centres, health centres, etc.

8. The existing damaged cross drainage structures and cross drainage
structures having insufficient vent way shall also be selected.

9, Normally, when the ground generally slopes from one side to another,
the embankment intercepts natural flow of rainwater. In such cases,
balancing culverts are provided at the rate of one per 500m length of
road to avoid water logging.

10. Selection of type of cross drainage structure for a particular road
primarily depends on the linear waterway and hydraulic particulars
apart from topography, catchment area, Cross-section of the stream at
proposed crossing, High Flood Level / Maximum Flood Level and Road
Top Level, etc.,

11. Span of the culvert, Minor Bridge and causeway shall be fixed based
on linear waterway of the channel/stream.

12. Generally, for catchment area of less than 1.25 sq km (125 hectares),
aculvert is required and if the catchment area is more than
1.25 sq km, a minor bridge is to be provided.

13. The District Collector should document various process of the Scheme
implementation starting from selection of works to completion of
works, documenting the works with photographs, video etc.,

(ii) Non-Negotiable Items:

1. Citizen Information Board shall be kept at the work site in 3 X 4 feet
with all necessary information as per the framework given by the
Government of India vide Ref No: k11023/1/1/2017 - MGNREGA (IV),
Ministry of Rural Development, Dated 07.04.2017. The Citizen
Information Board with wall writing shall be painted at Rs.850/-per
unit for Individual Asset and Rs.1,000 for Community Assets. The
Citizen Information Board (CIB) should only be wall painted for the
works like Panchayat Office Building, Dug wells, Food Grain Godown
and Milk Collection Centre.

2. Estimate creation, Administrative Sanction and Technical Sanction
shall be done in SECURE software except Panchayat Office Building
which is a convergence/ devolution work.

3. Geo-Tagging of Assets at all three i.e before, during and after
completion should be ensured.

4, The Government of India have notified the wage rate for the Financial
Year 2023-24 as Rs.294/- and the Rural Schedule of Rates has been
prepared accordingly for all the estimate preparation. In case of any
revision made in Rural Schedule of Rates in future, the same shall be
adopted for the works.

In addition to the above said guidelines, the following shall also be
adhered during execution of works;

e Resolution should be obtained in the Gram Sabha for all the
Selected Works/Beneficiaries Selected and it should be entered
work wise in Register No.2 of Mahatma Gandhi NREGA of village
panchayats concerned.

e Photographs shall be taken before execution, during execution and
after completion of work.

report to Government from time to time. The Financial sanction will be made
