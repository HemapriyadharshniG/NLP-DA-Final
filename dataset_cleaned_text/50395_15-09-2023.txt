I invite your kind attention to the reference cited.

Government has issued order for the construction of Working Sheds for
SHGs to provide common infrastructure facilities in rural areas with the aim to
provide proper capacity building development of skill for sustained livelihood
activities vide reference cited

The district wise target for 500 Working Shed has been communicated to
districts vide reference 2" cited. As per the model estimate approved in GO,
Working Shed should be constructed at a cost of Rs.9.60 Lakh with roofing
height of 4.2 meter to place and use the factory/heavy machineries.

However, based on the requirement at field level modification shall be
done for the type design and estimate amount communicated with GO.
Accordinly, the following instructions are issued.

e The roofing height of 3.6 meter shall be adopted for the activities
involving less SHG women like Garments and Jute Bags unit, Aari
Works, Flouring Machine Units Weaving unit, Making Bamboo and
Wire Basket, Terracotta Toys, Sanitary Napkins, Pot Making, Oil
Extracting Units and Handicraft Unit etc.,

e Only for the activities involving heavy machineries with the height 3
meter, the working sheds can be constructed with the roofing height of
4.2 meter.

e Turbo ventilator facilities should be provided in the roof.

e The provision for Toilet facility was not provided in the existing model.
Hence, a Mini CSC (Model-2) under SBM may be constructed for the
completed and ongoing Sheds based on the need. For the new type

designs also Toilets may be takenup under SBM if their available in the
District.
