I invite your attention to the references cited.
In the reference 4 cited, Government of India, Ministry of Rural

Development have approved Labour Budget of 20 Crore persondays under
MGNREGS to Tamil Nadu for the financial year 2023-24.

In the reference 5 cited, the Government of India have notified the wage
rate for the FY 2023-24 as Rs.294/- per day in respect of Tamil Nadu.

In the reference 6" cited, the Hon’ble Minister for Finance Department has
made the following Announcements on the floor of the Tamil Nadu Legislative

Assembly during the Demand of Rural Development & Panchayat Raj Department.

“Restoration of 6,618 water bodies has been taken up in rural areas
in the last two years at a cost of Rs.638 crore. Further, 10,000 water
bodies in rural areas consisting of minor irrigation tanks, ponds and

ooranies will be renovated in the next two years at a cost of Rs.800 crore.”

In continuation of the above, in the reference 7‘ cited, all the District
Collectors were requested to send the details of water bodies to be taken up in the
year 2023-24 that have not been taken up for renovation in the past 3 years under
any schemes.

Based on the reports as per reference 8, received from districts, it is arrived
that 7000 MI Tanks and Ponds and Ooranies will be taken up for renovation during
the year 2023-24.

Hence, I request the District Collectors to accord Administrative Sanction for

the renovation of MI Tanks, Ponds and Ooranies as per the Annexure enclosed.
