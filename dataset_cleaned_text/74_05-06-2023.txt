ORDER:

In the Gazette Notification first read above, the Government of
India notified revision of wage rate under MGNREGS for the year 2023-
2024 and pursuant to the above Notification in the Government Order
third read above, wage rate was revised from Rs.281/- to Rs.294/- per
person per day with effect from 01.04.2023 under Mahatma Gandhi
National Rura! Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS).

2. In the letter second read above, the Commissioner of Rural
Development and Panchayat Raj has stated that, from Financial Year
2019-20 onwards, estimate creation and administrative Sanction and
Technical Sanction under Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment
Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS) are being done using the SECURE
Software, which was developed and maintained by the National
Informatics Centre, Kerala, under the supervision and control of the
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India. Automatic updation
of the wage rate from Rs.281/- to Rs.294/- per day per person and
subsequent revision of rates in SECURE Software would take place on
1st April, 2023. Consequent on the adoption of new wage rate from

(p.7-6)

01.04.2023 in SECURE software, the Commissioner of Rural Development
and Panchayat Raj has sent a proposal for revised Schedule of Rates for
various types of activities to be executed in different types of soil,
plantation and differently abled persons for the Financial Year 2023-2024
under MGNREGS and requested the Government to approve the same.

3. The Government after careful examination of the proposal of the
Commissioner of Rural Development and Panchayat Raj, have decided to
accept the same and approve the revised Schedule of Rates for various
types of activities to be executed in different types of soil, plantation and
differently abled persons for the Financial Year 2023-24 under MGNREGS
as in the Annexure — I, II and III respectively annexed to this order.

4. This order is issued with the concurrence of Finance Department
vide its U.O.No.58/DS (RR)/RD/2023, dated 22.05.2023.
