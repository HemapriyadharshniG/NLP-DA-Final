Sub:- Reformation of NRM and Agriculture & Allied works in the MIS under

Madan/Sir,

This is regarding the reformation of Natural Resource Management (NRM) and
Agriculture & Allied works in the MIS of Mahatma Gandhi NREGS

2. Prior to FY 2023-24, the permissible works under the Scheme was arranged in
such a manner that, many works under Agriculture & Allied category were also
considered under the NRM category. Accordingly, the mapping of works in NREGASoft
database had been done in the similar manner with overlapping of works between two
categories i.e. Agriculture & Allied and NRM. Due to the overlap, the exact number of
permissible works under each categories cannot be derived, which results in the
overlap of data between both categories while drawing out physical and financial
progress/achievement.

3. To ensure the effective implementation & monitoring of the mandate of the
Mahatma Gandhi NREGA Act, an exercise has been carried out to understand the 266
permissible works bifurcation into three broad heads i.e. NRM, Agri & allied and Rural
Infrastructure works. This bifurcation is ensuring no overlap between any head and
the same has been mapped with the data base The number of works under each
broad head is as following:

Broad head Number of works
i. Natural Resource Management (NRM) - 58
i. Agriculture & allied - 150
iii. Rural Infrastructure - 58
Total . 266

Contd/

4. As per the Mahatma Gandhi NREG Act, at least 60% of the works taken up in a
district, in terms of cost, shall be for creation of productive assets directly linked to
agriculture and allied activities through development of land, water & trees and as per
the joint guideline on Mission Water Conservation (MWC), 65% of the total expenditure
in a water stressed block (MWC block) should be made on NRM related works.

5. _ The list of permissible work related to NRM, Agri & allied and Rural infrastructure
has been attached here with for reference and the States are requested to carry out
the works according to the above three distinguished categories.
