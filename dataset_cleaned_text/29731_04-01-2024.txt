I invite your attention to the reference cited.

In the reference cited, detailed circular has been issued to all Districts
regarding implementation of MGNREGS. In this circular, it is instructed that
AC(D)/ PD, EE(RD), AEE, APO, BDO (VP) and Zonal Deputy.BDOs to inspect
MGNREGS works continuously as per the target given.

SI.No Name of the Officer Targeted VPs for inspection
1. AC(D)/PD 10 Village panchayats (20 works)
2 Executive Engineer 12 Village panchayats (24 works)
3 APO(WE) / AEE(SD) 15 Village panchayats (30 works)
4. BDO(VP) ; 20 Village panchayats (40 works)
5 Zonal Dy.BDO All Village panchayats (All works)

In this regard, the following Inspection formats are enclosed herewith for

action.

1. Officer Inspection formats

2. Work Inspection —- NRM Works

3. Work Inspection - Rural Infrastructure Works
4. Work Inspection — Individual Asset Works

Hence, I request you to follow the above inspection formats during
inspection and the format is tentative only. During inspection, the required

points may be added or unnecessary points may be ignored depending upon

the works inspected. But the inspection reports to be fulfilled all the details
covered in the above inspection formats. Remarks / Suggestion should be filled

in the inspection reports.
